Three things the audit did not catch

Found while building. Worth knowing, because two of them were worse than the list said.

  • Crew had no Plan tab at all

    The audit said crew were missing a staff section. In fact canSeePlan was isHost || isApprovedMember, and staff are neither — so a guide opening the trip they work on saw only the public Overview. Now gated on roster.view too.

  • The Members screen was entirely host-gated

    Giving managers the pending-requests count in Plan was not enough: the screen that count leads to still refused them. Join requests now go by trip.edit and removing a traveler by travelers.remove — separate capabilities, the way the database separates them.

  • A bug introduced and fixed the same hour

    Handing staff the Plan tab also handed them a floating "Request to Join" on their own trip — which the database refuses outright (staff and travelers are exclusive). A button that always errors. Gated off.

4

Scenario review — the paths one trip takes

night pass · 17 aug
hole

A cancelled trip still takes money

Cancelling hides the buttons and notifies everyone — but payments-checkout never reads the trip's status. A traveler on a stale screen or an old push link can still pay a cancelled trip, and the server says yes. One status = 'active' check.

hole

Deadline reminders never fire on their own

The due-soon and overdue notifications exist and render — but their only producer is the operator pressing "Remind" on the dashboard. The daily job covers trip start and end, not requirements. A traveler can sail past every document and payment deadline in silence, and the operator is not told either.

hole

A chargeback is invisible

The webhook handles "paid" and "refunded", nothing else. A traveler disputes a card charge → no row is written, nobody is told, and every money total is silently wrong from that day on. Disputes can arrive up to ~18 months after the charge.

hole

Stripe breaking is silent

When an operator's account becomes ready, they get a notification. When it becomes disabled — papers past due, payouts frozen — nothing fires. The operator finds out when a traveler's payment fails, which is the worst possible messenger.

hole

Removing a paid traveler leaves the money behind

Remove works: it notifies them, cleans up the chat, deletes the join request. But nothing points at the refund — that is a separate manual step the operator has to remember. Same for someone kicked mid-onboarding after paying the deposit.

partly

Deposit paid, onboarding abandoned — and nobody notices18 aug

Built. The traveler gets three nudges — 24 hours, 3 days, 7 days — and then silence; they paid, so a reminder is welcome, but money held does not buy unlimited pushes. Each one names the steps actually blocking them, because someone who stopped because they were unsure which step it was is not helped by "you have steps left".

The operator gets one digest per trip, never one push per stuck traveler, and after a week the trip page carries it as a to-do — the point where all three nudges have gone unanswered and there is nothing left to try automatically.

The clock runs from the traveler's last action, not the deposit, so someone steadily working a long list is never nagged. Database side is applied and the dashboard to-do works today; the two edge functions and the cron are still to deploy, which is what makes the pushes start.

closed

A new waiver quietly un-signs everyone18 aug

Closed by removing the ability, not by adding a notification. The waiver freezes the moment anyone is affected — but stays swappable while the trip is empty, so an operator who uploaded the wrong PDF is not stuck with it.

"Empty" is two tests, and the second is the one that matters: no travelers, and no signatures from anyone. A member count alone would have been wrong twice over — every trip carries its own host as a member, so the door would never open; and crew sign the same document without being members, so a trip with zero travelers can still have a signed photographer. A trigger checks both inside the write, so someone joining while the operator picks a file loses the race safely.

hole

Moving the dates moves every deadline in silence

Due dates are counted back from the start date, so changing it reschedules every document and the final payment — and there is no "trip changed" notification at all. Travelers find out if the operator presses Remind.

holds

Refund math is self-correcting

Refunds land as negative payment rows, so every "paid" number in the app, the dashboard and the server subtracts them by plain sum. Partial and repeated refunds compute the delta from Stripe's cumulative figure, so a redelivered webhook cannot double-count.

holds

Over-approving is safe end to end

Approving takes no seat. The seat is claimed at the end of onboarding under a row lock, and checkout refuses the deposit when the trip is already full — before charging. The operator can approve 14 people for 10 spots and the boat fills in finish order.

holds

Repricing never touches an existing deal

Changing the trip's price pins everyone already on it to their current price first, then reprices, then syncs the pay rows — and if the pinning fails, the new price is not written. A per-traveler price set by hand is never second-guessed by anything.

holds

Cancel, leave, remove and decline all notify — from any client

They are database triggers, not app code, so cancelling from the web dashboard fires the same notifications as cancelling from the phone.

holds

Refunds still work on a cancelled trip

The one payment path that must stay open after cancelling, and it does — checked deliberately, since the checkout hole above is the same missing status read in the other direction.

holds

Signatures are evidence, not checkmarks

Waiver and policy consents store the exact text version, a hash, the typed name, IP and timestamp — enough to answer "what exactly did they agree to, and when" years later.

Deliberately not done

None of these was on your list. They are either known gaps from the staff spec or a second pass — worth a decision, not a surprise.

  • Staff get no notifications

    The fan-out builds its admin list from participants with the host role and does not know the staff table exists. An invited guide is never told, and a manager misses every document and payment notification. Its own piece of work.

  • Staff are not in the group chat

    The chat capability is granted by the Guide and Manager tiers and wired to nothing — a guide needs a conversation membership, which is a separate permission system. So the chat button stays host and member only, on purpose: a button that errors is worse than no button.

  • Inviting travelers is still host-only

    A manager reviews join requests and removes people, but does not invite. Not asked for — say the word and it is one gate.

  • The web operator dashboard is untouched

    Its Crew page already did the document review the app just gained. Nothing there regressed, and nothing there gained the new capabilities either.
