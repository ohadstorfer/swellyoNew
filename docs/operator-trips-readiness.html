Blockers

A real operator's first trip breaks on these
1

Stripe is still in test mode

No real money has ever moved through this. The database holds 10 test payments and 0 live ones.

Four switches flip together: the sk_live_ key in the edge functions, app.stripe_livemode in the database, EXPO_PUBLIC_STRIPE_LIVEMODE in the app, VITE_STRIPE_LIVEMODE on the dashboard. Miss one and nothing errors — the totals just quietly disagree with each other.

2

Cancelling a trip refunds nobody

cancelTrip() sets status = 'cancelled' and stops. Travelers who paid keep their money with the operator and get a banner.

This is the worst day an operator can have, and right now it is handled one refund at a time, by hand, by you.

3

The operator agreement is a summary, not a contract

Operators accept summary-2026-08-12. It says nothing about an insurance minimum, indemnity, licence, or a duty to refund.

The machinery is right — versioned text, timestamp, re-ask on bump. Only the words are missing. The code comment already says so: "when the real agreement lands, bump this once more."

✓

A late joiner can hold a seat while owing most of the pricedone

A participant row created on or after the full-payment deadline now freezes deposit_usd at the whole price. One payment, in onboarding, for the full cost — and the balance works out to zero on its own.

Applied to production. Proved in a rolled-back transaction: with the deadline 20 days past a new traveler froze at 3000 / 3000; with it still ahead, 3000 / 1000. Nobody who already joined moved. The screen says "Full payment" instead of "Deposit".

✓

Nothing is committeddone

All 82 files are in, as four commits: the operator trips work, the Swelly message caps, the health-check timeout fix, and the late-joiner migration.

Tests and typecheck match the baseline at HEAD exactly — same 3 pre-existing failing suites before and after, nothing new.

Fix soon

Works today, will hurt with real customers

The traveler gets nothing in writing after paying

A person pays $1,000 and we send no receipt. payments-checkout never sets receipt_email, so not even Stripe's own receipt goes out.

The operator is not told when someone pays

stripe-webhook records the money and sends zero notifications. The operator finds out by opening the dashboard and looking.

A traveler who paid can just leave

leaveTrip() removes them from the trip. No refund, no warning about the policy they agreed to, no note to the operator that a paid seat opened up.

Becoming an operator is a manual database call

set_operator_status() is admin-only, by design. Fine for one operator. It is the whole bottleneck at ten.

No money view across trips

The dashboard shows money one trip at a time. An operator running five trips has no total, no upcoming payouts, no "who owes me what" in one place.

Already works

Checked against the live schema

Operator setup

  • Six steps, and all six are editable later in Settings
  • Stripe Connect onboarding, with all six waiting states handled
  • Payout status kept live — charges, payouts, what Stripe still needs
  • Default currency, cancellation policy, waiver, insurance certificate
  • Terms acceptance is versioned and stamped server-side

Selling a trip

  • Price, deposit, and a full-payment deadline counted back from the start date
  • Card payments or offline — the operator picks per trip
  • Prices shown in the traveler's own currency, charged in USD
  • The operator is the merchant of record; their name is on the statement
  • 12% commission, taken automatically
  • Price and cancel switch are locked to the operator — a manager cannot touch them

Travelers

  • Request → approve → onboarding, and approval takes no seat
  • Documents, waiver, medical form, passport with the APIS fields
  • A deadline per requirement, with a daily reminder job running
  • The cancellation policy is frozen on the trip and shown before paying
  • Consent stored with the exact text, a hash, IP and timestamp
  • Rejected files are deleted from storage, not just marked
  • Passports are purged on a schedule

Money

  • Checkout, partial payments, deposit and balance as separate debts
  • Refunds from the app and from the dashboard
  • Refunds give our commission back too, so the operator is not out of pocket
  • Test and live payments never mix into the same total
  • Refunds are gated on one capability, checked in the database

Crew

  • Five tiers, and the permissions are database rows — not a release
  • Invite by link or in-app, with paperwork attached to the invite
  • Crew can be asked for their own documents and medical form
  • Crew and travelers can never be the same person
  • Crew show on the trip page with photo, title and bio

The web dashboard

  • Eleven pages — trips, one trip, money, crew, waiting list, documents, travelers, settings
  • Adds nothing to the database; every read is already permitted
  • Never indexed, never framed, no referrer leaked
  • Warns when it finds payments in the Stripe mode it is not counting

Your call, not code

Nothing below is a bug
?

Who holds the money until the trip runs?

Today: nobody. A $1,000 deposit reaches the operator's Stripe balance months before anyone gets on a plane. If they disappear, the money is already gone. Stripe can hold it — that is a setting and a decision, not a build.

?

Is 12% the number?

It is the database default and no operator has agreed to it in writing. It becomes much harder to change after the first one signs.

?

What happens when an operator cancels?

Who refunds, out of whose balance, and by when. Answer this before building blocker 2 — the code follows the policy, not the other way round.

?

Do we check the insurance, or just keep it?

We store the certificate. Nobody reads it, and nothing checks the cover amount or the expiry date.

?

The seven lawyer questions are still open

Jurisdiction, package-travel rules, whether our waiver can release Swellyo as well as the operator. They are written out in agreements-and-terms.md §7 and none has an answer.

What is left, in this order

Get the agreement written and bump the terms version. Longest lead time, and nothing else waits on you to start it.

Answer the cancellation question, then build refund-on-cancel and the payment receipt together — they are the same afternoon.

Flip Stripe last, all four switches in one sitting, and run one real trip of your own through it before an outside operator sees it.
