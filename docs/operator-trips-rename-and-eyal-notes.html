Eyal's notes, translated

On req_type

מדיקל יהיה אקנולדג׳? אולי צריך להיות fill

"Will medical be an acknowledge? Maybe it should be fill."

He is right that it is a poor fit. acknowledge means "I read this and agree" — that is the waiver. Medical is a form you type answers into. Adding a fourth type fill is a one-line constraint change and would stop medical borrowing a type that does not describe it. I would do it.

On title

מה זה?

"What is this?"

It is the label the traveler reads on the row — "Passport", "Sign waiver", "Travel insurance". It exists because of custom requirements: for the six built-in kinds we could hardcode the label, but when an operator adds their own item they have to type its name somewhere. help_text is the optional line underneath.

On the passport fields

לא הבנתי מה זה

"I didn't understand what this is."

When the 30-day purge deletes a passport photo, we keep three typed values off it: name as printed, nationality, expiry date. Two reasons. A repeat traveler does not retype them next trip — they just retake the photo. And we can warn someone whose passport expires before their trip. The photo is gone; three text fields survive.

On the acknowledgements table — needs your decision

אמרנו שמוחקים את כל זה

"We said we're deleting all of this."

I did not drop it, and I want to flag why before we do. That table is the only record that a specific person agreed to a specific waiver version, with their name and a timestamp. It is the entire legal output of the waiver feature — delete it and someone signs a waiver and nothing anywhere remembers it.

If the intent is to fold it into organized_trip_travelers_documents (one row per traveler per requirement, whether it is a file, an agreement, or a filled form), that is a clean design and it pairs naturally with his fill idea — but it needs storage_path to become nullable and three columns added. Tell me which and I will do it in one migration.
