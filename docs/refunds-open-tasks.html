Why Phase 3 matters the day Stripe goes live

A refund is the operator choosing to give money back — that works now, and it comes out of his balance. A chargeback is the traveler phoning their own bank, which takes the money by force. Stripe debits that from Swellyo's account — with or without on_behalf_of. On a $1,000 trip: Swellyo ends at about −$895, the operator keeps +$880. Recovering it is code we have not written yet.
