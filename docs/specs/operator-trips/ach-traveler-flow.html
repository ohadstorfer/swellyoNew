What a traveller goes through when they pay by bank

A card confirms while they watch. A bank transfer doesn't — the money takes up to four business days, and the app has to carry them through the wait without either alarming them or inviting a second payment. This is that journey, step by step, with exactly what the screen says at each point.

Steps for the traveller
1 vs 4
How long, exactly. Stripe's standard ACH settlement is T+4 — up to four business days from the payment, cutoff 21:00 US/Eastern. Stripe also offers T+2 for eligible US accounts, and on Connect the platform controls the speed for every operator — one toggle in Settings → Payment methods. Weekends and US bank holidays don't count. In the sandbox it takes seconds.
Why it's off. A week of limbo on a $1,000 deposit isn't a payment method, it's a support ticket. A traveller whose bank can't do instant login should pay by card — the option is right there. Checkout now sends verification_method: instant, so the manual link no longer appears.
What the test found. That microdeposit attempt completed Checkout with a status our webhook silently ignored, then failed 20 seconds later. The silence is fixed (it now logs the status and event id), and the path is closed. The $200 "Final payment" attempt it left behind is correctly recorded as failed — nothing was charged.

How to run the test now

Log in as Sababa → El Salvador 26 → Deposit → US bank account → Test (Non-OAuth) bank tile → pick the "Success" checking account → Pay $1,000. In the sandbox the day-3 event arrives within a minute or two, so you'll see the whole journey compressed: the calm sheet, the "On its way" row, then the 🎉 push and the tick.

To see the bounce instead: same steps, but choose the "Insufficient funds" account in the test bank.
