Trip currency display — spec & plan

10 August 2026 · rev 4 · operator trips (type C)

What we are building, in one breath.
  • The operator types the price in their currency (₪ $ € £).
  • Every traveler sees prices in their own currency — auto from their country, with a picker to override.
  • Everyone still pays in US$. The app says so clearly, the Booking.com way.
  • Every price is a clean number. The caveat is words at checkout, not a symbol on the figure.

Rev 3 — what changed while building it 10 deltas from the plan

Better than planned:

  • Rounding solved properly. Neither rev-1 coarse steps nor plain conversion. New snapToRound snaps to the coarsest round number within the known round-trip drift, so ₪4,501→₪4,500 but ₪4,550 stays ₪4,550. Safe by construction, not by taste.
  • Root cause removed. fromTripCurrency now keeps cents. Whole-dollar storage was what made ₪4,500 come back as ₪4,501 in the first place.
  • Payment surfaces got simpler, not more complex. Because hints use the daily rate, budgetFxRate/viewerCountry prop threading was deleted from PayAmountSheet, PlanSections and 3 call sites — they call useViewer() directly.

Decisions the plan didn't cover:

  • Operator currency is LOCKED in edit mode. Switching after publish would reinterpret the frozen rate and silently re-price everyone already joined.
  • Switching currency mid-create CONVERTS what's typed. Leaving "4500" alone when ₪→$ is tapped would redefine it as $4,500 — a 3.5× price change with no visible cause.
  • USD trips now store budget_fx_rate = 1, not the ILS rate. A USD row carrying an ILS number is exactly the ambiguity the display rule has to defend against.
  • TravelerPriceSheet follows the TRIP's currency, not the operator's country. An Israeli operator who priced a trip in € negotiates in €.
  • 14 currencies, not 13 — added NZD. AUD without NZD was a real gap.

Deliberate deviation from the spec:

  • Hand-rolled symbol table, not Intl.NumberFormat. Hermes resolves Intl against the platform's ICU, so the same amount renders "$1,200" on iOS, "US$1,200" on Android and a third way in jest. Prices sit next to each other in lists and must be byte-identical. The spec's intent (right symbol, right decimals) lives in the table.

Bugs found and fixed on the way:

  • The create-flow PREVIEW priced ₪ as $. It passed the raw typed number through as USD, so a ₪4,500 trip previewed as "$4,500". Never caught because it only shows pre-publish.
  • Two more 3.0 call sites beyond the four in the plan — BudgetTierCards and BudgetTierCardsBig. Six total, all gone.
  • Late-loading profile defaulted operators to USD. The old code re-derived every render; making it state exposed the race. Fixed with an adopt-until-touched effect.
Files: 20 modified, 4 new (useViewer.ts, ratesService.ts, CurrencySheet.tsx, the migration). ~1,100 insertions.
Rev 2 — what the self-review changed 4 changes, 2 holds partly superseded by rev 4

Historical record. Every "≈" decision below was overturned in rev 4 — prices carry no marker at all.

I re-checked the spec against Booking.com's real behavior and independent UX research. Verbatim, their checkout line is:

"This price is converted to show you the approximate cost in US$. You'll pay in €. The exchange rate may change before you pay."

Three promises: it's approximate · here's the real currency · the rate can move. Rev 1 had the first two and missed the third. Changes:

  • Changed — estimate rounding. Rev 1 rounded estimates to coarse steps (€10, ¥1,000) — that's Apple's way, not Booking's. Booking shows the precise converted amount (whole units) and marks it approximate. Now: estimates are exact whole-unit conversions with "≈". Coarse steps survive only where they recover the operator's typed price (§2 case 1).
  • Changed — payment-surface copy. Now uses all three Booking clauses, including "the exchange rate may change before you pay."
  • Changed — payment-surface hints. Always "≈", even in the trip's own currency, and computed at the daily rate, not the trip's frozen one — at pay time the honest estimate is what the bank will do today, not what the operator froze months ago.
  • Changed — found a missed surface. The Explore budget chips (TripsScreen.tsx:861) build their ₪ label with the hardcoded 3.0 fallback. Added to the sweep; another 3.0 call site dies.
  • Held — auto + override. Research agrees: auto-detection alone gets people wrong (Baymard: 18% abandon without local currency; geolocation misfires are the known failure), so a manual switcher is required. Our "profile country + settings override" is the textbook answer.
  • Held — "≈" on cards. Booking's cards omit a marker and rely on a site-wide disclaimer. We keep the one-character "≈". OVERTURNED in rev 4 — see the card above. Booking shows no marker and neither do we.
Sources: Booking.com checkout wording (user-documented) · Booking.com currency behavior · Baymard research · auto-detect + manual switcher guidance
Decisions locked with Ohad (10 Aug): auto currency from profile country plus a settings override · ~10+ display currencies at launch · operator picks the input currency from a short list. Booking.com itself fixes the currency by property location — that doesn't fit surf trips (nobody prices a Morocco camp in MAD), so the operator picks instead.
Part 1 · Spec

1 · The three currencies of one trip

RoleWhat it isWhere it lives
Stored currency Always USD. Every amount column stays USD. Nothing changes here. group_trips.cost_per_person, budget_min/max, deposit_amount, trip_payment_events.amount_usd
Trip currency What the operator typed the price in. Frozen rate, set once, never re-frozen on edit. budget_currency (text, no constraint — verified live) + budget_fx_rate (now means: units of trip currency per 1 USD)
Viewer currency What the traveler sees. Auto from country, or their override. New: surfers.display_currency (null = auto)

2 · What a price looks like — the display rule

One function decides every browse price. Three cases, checked in order:

  1. Viewer currency = trip currency → show the operator's real price (via the trip's frozen rate). An Israeli looking at an Israeli trip sees the exact ₪ the operator meant. This is what happens today for ₪ — it stays. snapToRound undoes round-trip drift: the operator typed ₪4,500, stored USD gives back ₪4,501, and it restores ₪4,500 — but never moves a number further than that drift, so a real ₪4,550 stays ₪4,550.
  2. Viewer currency = USD → show the exact $ amount. This IS the charge currency.
  3. Anything else → convert USD at the cached daily rate and show the precise whole-unit amount, clean and unmarked (the Booking.com way — see rev 4). No rate available → fall back to plain $.
Trip priced ₪4,500 by an Israeli operator (stored: $1,286, rate 3.5, currency ILS):

Israeli viewer  ₪4,500 — the operator's real price
US viewer  $1,286 — the charge currency
French viewer  €1,187 — precise conversion at today's rate
No rate cached  $1,286 — never a guessed number

Payment surfaces play by a stricter rule

  • The headline number is always the exact USD being charged (formatExactUsd stays; never converted, never rounded).
  • The local-currency hint reads "about ₪X" — even for the trip's own currency — and uses the daily rate, not the trip's frozen one. The frozen rate says what the operator meant months ago; the bank charges at today's. Booking.com's estimate at checkout is today's rate for the same reason.
  • One line, Booking.com's three clauses in our words: "The ₪ price is approximate. You'll pay in US$. The exchange rate may change before you pay."
  • One price per screen. The hint is smaller and grey, never equal weight with the real number.

  • 13 display currencies + USD. Adding one later = one row in the map; the rates API returns every currency in a single call.
  • Estimates are whole units in every currency — no decimals, no coarse steps, no marker.
  • Deliberately skipped: ARS, TRY and other fast-moving currencies — a daily rate can be wrong by evening, and a wrong price is worse than $.

4 · Operator input (create flow)

  • The price step gets a small currency selector: ₪ · $ · € · £. Default pre-picked from the operator's country (Israel → ₪, eurozone → €, UK → £, else $).
  • Input list is short on purpose — it's what real surf operators price in. The display list (13) is separate and longer.
  • On save: convert typed amount → USD at today's rate; store budget_currency + budget_fx_rate. Exactly the ₪ mechanism today, two more currencies.
  • Edit never re-freezes. The existing saveRate pattern (edit reuses the trip's stored rate) stays law. It already prevented a re-pricing bug once.
  • The input field shows the chosen symbol (fixes the known "₪ input with a $ icon" minor).

5 · The exchange rate — where it comes from

No new DB table, no cron. The client fetches ALL rates in one free call (open.er-api.com/v6/latest/USD — already used by the create flow), caches them in AsyncStorage with a timestamp, refreshes when older than 12h, and keeps the last good copy forever.
  • FALLBACK_USD_TO_ILS = 3.0 dies. The fallback chain is now: fresh rate → last good cached rate → show USD. Not because 3.0 is far off — USD/ILS is 2.9987 today, so it is nearly right — but because it is a constant pretending to be a live number, and nothing signals when it drifts. Call sites: currency.ts, CreateTripFlowA (×3), the Explore budget chips, and both Budget-tier cards. Six total.
  • Rate ladder by use: display estimates → cached daily rate; trip's own currency, browse → the trip's frozen rate, exact; payment hints → daily rate, worded "about"; operator save → fresh fetch, else cached if ≤48h old, else block non-USD input with a friendly error ("We can't get today's rate — try again or price in $"). A wrong rate frozen into a trip is forever.
  • First launch offline: no cache → everything shows $ — correct, never wrong.
  • Two devices may cache slightly different daily rates. Fine for estimates — the charge is USD either way.

6 · Viewer override (settings)

  • New row in SettingsScreen.tsx: "Currency · Auto (₪)". Opens a BottomSheetShell list: Auto (recommended) + the 14 currencies.
  • Saved to surfers.display_currency (null = auto) so it follows the user across devices — Booking.com likewise remembers "the last currency you used".
  • Auto = country → currency map from country_from. Unknown country → USD. Auto-detection alone is not enough (it misfires); the override is what makes it safe — this is the finding behind "auto + override".

7 · Scope & non-goals

  • Display applies to all group trips — the formatter is shared; a type-C-only rule would show ₪ on one card and € on the next. Payment copy applies to type C surfaces (only type C takes payments).
  • Not in this project: charging in local currency (Stripe Adaptive Pricing — separate, later step), operator payouts in local currency, changing anything about the money pipeline. payments-checkout stays 100% untouched.
  • Notifications and push copy keep USD amounts for now (payment reminders etc.). Localizing amounts inside templates is a follow-up, not this project.
  • All client work is JS-only → OTA-able. No native, no rebuild.
  • Operator dashboard stays USD-only for now (it adds nothing to the DB by its own rule; its exports already say USD).

8 · Facts verified against the live DB & code (10 Aug)

  • budget_currency is text, nullable, no CHECK constraint — EUR/GBP need no column migration.
  • Live explore_feed / my_trips_feed return budget_fx_rate but NOT budget_currency — cards can't apply rule 1 without it → both RPCs must change (Part 2, step 1).
  • surfers has no currency column — display_currency is new.
  • Explore budget chips hardcode the 3.0 fallback into their ₪ label (TripsScreen.tsx:861).
Part 2 · Plan

Six steps, in order. Steps 2–6 are OTA-able client work. Step 1 is the only DB touch, applied manually by Ohad in the SQL editor.

Step 1 — Database migration manual, SQL editor one migration file
  • Write supabase/migrations/<ts>_trip_display_currency.sql:
  • a. ALTER TABLE surfers ADD COLUMN display_currency text; (nullable, no default, optional CHECK on the 14 codes).
  • b. Add budget_currency to both card RPCs — explore_feed and my_trips_feed: RETURNS TABLE and inner SELECT.

⚠️ The RPC dance (this bit a whole debugging session last time):

  • First diff the live definitions with pg_get_functiondef — live is ahead of the repo; never start from the repo copy.
  • RETURNS TABLE change needs DROP + CREATE, not CREATE OR REPLACE.
  • Recreate re-grants PUBLIC → re-REVOKE from PUBLIC/anon, re-GRANT to authenticated, re-pin search_path.
  • Check the SQL editor is on project rfdhtvcmagsbxqntnepv before running.

Old clients ignore the new column — safe to apply before the client ships.

Detail screens use select('*') so they get budget_currency already — "detail right, cards wrong" is the symptom if this step is skipped.

Order & risk

  • Order: 1 (DB, safe early) → 2 → 3 → 4/5/6 in any order. 2+3 are the heart; 4–6 are each small and independent.
  • Biggest risk: the RPC recreate (step 1b). It's the one place with a bad history — the migration file must contain the REVOKE/GRANT/search_path block, written from the live definition.
  • Quiet regression to watch: Israelis are the users with an opinion about today's behavior. Case 2 keeps the one ILS-priced trip pixel-identical for them; the visible change is on $-priced trips, which now convert at today's rate in whole units instead of the frozen rate rounded to ₪100 — deliberate, Booking-style.
  • Rollout: one OTA after all steps land. No native gate anywhere in this project.
